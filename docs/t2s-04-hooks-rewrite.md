## First, why version one was weak

You were right, and your own vault explains exactly why. It ranks the layers by leverage:

> "The offer moves CPL 3 to 5x. The creative moves it 30 to 50%."
> "The angle is the ceiling."
> Hook rate is the audition. Hold rate is the performance.

Your offer is already strong. Published prices plus an instant estimator are the two top ranked offers in the entire home improvement vertical. So the offer was not the problem.

The problem was **angle and first frame.** Version one ran five ads on basically two angles, Mechanism and Price, both aimed at a solution-aware buyer. Your vault's 9 by 5 grid says that is 40 ads sitting in three cells wondering why reach plateaued. And every hook I wrote was a **verbal** hook. The vault is explicit that a pattern interrupt is physical, not verbal, and that:

> "every hook variant must have a different first frame, not just different overlay text."

I gave you copy. You needed openings.

**One honest pushback on "attention hacking."** Your vault argues against the shock version of it, twice. Barry Hott: "Blend in to stand out. Users skip anything that pattern-matches to ad." And the hook failure table: a 35 percent hook rate with a curiosity hook that has nothing to do with the offer produces a CTR under 0.8 percent, which is expensive impressions. The vault's version of attention hacking is **a different first frame, a real number, and a local marker.** Everything below is built on that, not on gimmicks.

## Angle: Objection handling
### The flaky quote angle. Your whitespace.

**H1. Problem agitate**

> ES: "No se quedó con la bañera porque le guste. Se quedó porque los últimos dos nunca le devolvieron la llamada."

## How to read the test

Day 4 or 5, look at **hook rate only.** Custom column in Ads Manager: 3 second video plays divided by impressions.

| Band | Read |
|---|---|
| Under 15% | Kill it today. The opening frame failed. |
| 15 to 25% | Swap the hook, keep the body. |
| 25 to 35% | Healthy. Keep it if hold rate holds. |
| 35%+ | Scale it and make five versions. |

Then and only then look at hold rate, which is 15 second plays divided by 3 second plays. Healthy is 15 to 25 percent. The vault: "Hook rate is the audition. Hold rate is the performance. Fix hook first, then hold, then CTR. Skip a level and you mis-diagnose for weeks."

If a hook hits 35 percent and CTR is under 0.8 percent, that hook is entertaining and irrelevant. Make it about the job, not about the shock.

# Nextdoor Tub-to-Shower Visual

## Delivery

- Format: PNG
- Canvas: 1200 x 1200 px, 1:1
- Placement: Nextdoor image ad or Business Page post
- Generation mode: Built-in image generation

## Final prompt

Create a finished 1:1 Nextdoor image ad for Broke & Fixed Home Solutions. Show the same modest Miami-Dade suburban bathroom from one consistent doorway angle in a believable before-and-after transformation. The left side is an outdated almond-beige bathtub with dated tile, a bulky shower curtain, worn grout, and a cramped feel. The right side replaces the tub in the identical footprint with a bright walk-in shower, low threshold, clear frameless glass, warm-white stone-look wall tile, a recessed niche, and brushed nickel fixtures. Use photorealistic renovation photography, accurate plumbing and construction details, natural window light, true material texture, straight vertical lines, and a 24mm eye-level view. Keep all important content within the central 80 percent safe zone.

Use navy #1E3A5F, orange #F07A1A, cream #F8F9FC, and green only for the graphic layout. Render this copy exactly: "DITCH THE TUB." "STEP INTO A BETTER SHOWER." "BEFORE" "AFTER" "TUB-TO-SHOWER CONVERSIONS" "CALL (786) 363-7039" "BROKE & FIXED HOME SOLUTIONS".

Create a premium but approachable social ad with strong phone-size legibility. No people, invented logos, watermarks, warped fixtures, impossible plumbing, duplicate faucets, fisheye distortion, luxury spa styling, tiny text, extra words, misspellings, prohibited business-language terms, or dash punctuation.

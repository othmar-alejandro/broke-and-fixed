# Tub-to-Shower Programmatic SEO Research

**Campaign:** Tub-to-shower conversion
**Market:** Kendall corridor and nearby South Miami communities, Florida
**Research date:** August 11, 2026
**Primary conversion URL:** `/en/landing/tub-to-shower`
**Spanish conversion URL:** `/es/landing/tub-to-shower`

## Executive recommendation

Build a small, useful intent cluster around the existing conversion page. Do not publish one thin page for every city. The current search results already contain many generic local service pages. The better opportunity is to answer the planning questions that appear repeatedly in the results, then send ready readers to the price-range form.

The first launch includes three guide pages:

1. `tub-to-shower-conversion-cost-miami`
2. `tub-to-shower-conversion-timeline-miami`
3. `tub-to-shower-conversion-for-seniors-miami`

It also includes two supporting posts:

1. `tub-to-shower-conversion-checklist-miami`
2. `tub-to-shower-design-ideas-miami`

The existing broad pages remain important:

- `/guides/bathtub-to-shower-conversion`, broad project explanation
- `/guides/walk-in-shower-conversion-miami`, broad local service page
- `/blog/walk-in-shower-vs-bathtub-miami`, decision and resale intent

## What the SERP research showed

The Exa search pass covered local service results, national cost pages, FAQ pages, and decision content. The results do not provide reliable monthly volume, so this is a qualitative opportunity map, not a volume forecast.

Repeated commercial phrases:

- tub to shower conversion Miami
- bathtub to shower conversion Miami
- tub to shower conversion near me
- walk-in shower conversion Miami
- replace tub with shower Miami
- shower conversion estimate Miami-Dade
- tub removal and shower installation

Repeated planning questions:

- How much does a tub-to-shower conversion cost?
- How long does the conversion take?
- Can the new shower use the same footprint?
- Does the drain need to move?
- Should I remove the only tub in my home?
- Is a walk-in shower safer for older adults?
- What type of glass and tile should I choose?
- Can the project be done without remodeling the whole bathroom?

Repeated local modifiers:

- Miami
- Miami-Dade
- South Florida
- Kendall
- Palmetto Bay
- West Kendall
- Doral
- The Hammocks
- Coral Gables
- Pinecrest
- Cutler Bay

The local SERP language leans heavily on “walk-in shower,” “same footprint,” “low entry,” “frameless glass,” “safety,” “free estimate,” and “one to two days.” Broke & Fixed should use those phrases where they are accurate, but the site should keep its own realistic five to eight working day range for a tiled conversion rather than repeating a faster system promise that may describe a different scope.

## Internal linking rules

Every new guide and post should link to the landing page with descriptive anchor text such as “tub-to-shower conversion estimate” or “Miami tub-to-shower project range.” Avoid using the same generic “click here” anchor everywhere.

The landing page should link back to the cost, timeline, safety, and checklist pages from relevant sections. Existing bathroom and location pages can link to the broad conversion guide, not every new page. This keeps the cluster understandable and prevents 17 near-identical location pages from competing with one another.

Recommended hub relationships:

```text
Landing page
├── Cost guide
├── Timeline guide
├── Senior-friendly safety guide
├── Conversion checklist post
└── Small bathroom design post
```

## On-page requirements for every programmatic guide

- One H1 matching the page intent, not a repeated city template
- Unique title and meta description in English and Spanish
- Canonical and hreflang through the existing guide route
- Article, FAQ, and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD through the existing guide route
- Three or more useful sections with Miami-Dade context
- FAQ answers written as complete answers, not one-line keyword blocks
- One direct link to `/landing/tub-to-shower`
- Links to two or three related guides
- Clear price language that says a range is not a firm quote

## Content and trust notes

The company is fully insured and family owned. Keep the copy direct and practical. Avoid generic sales language. Explain what is included, what can change the budget, and what a homeowner should ask before demolition.

The current landing page already has the strongest conversion action, a three-step price-range form. Supporting pages should educate first, then send high-intent readers there. The PDF guide stays a secondary option for lower-intent visitors.

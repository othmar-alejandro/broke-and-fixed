# Competitor Gap Analysis, May 20, 2026

## Executive Summary

- **Build hyperlocal location pages now.** Trimline ranks for Pinecrest, Coral Gables, Palmetto Bay, and Kendall by naming each city in H1 + body copy. None of the six competitors have true `/city/service` matrix pages, only Trimline mentions cities. This is the biggest open lane.
- **Lead with proof numbers in the hero, like MIDI.** "315 projects, 21 years, 547 homes remodeled, since 1999" stat blocks pull weight. Broke & Fixed's current hero has no comparable counters.
- **Add Aggregate Rating + Review schema with first-party reviews on the homepage and every service page.** Mauro's, MIDI, and J&J have Google review badges but no on-page Review JSON-LD. This is a fast win, Broke & Fixed can leapfrog them.
- **Tighten the offer.** MIDI says "FREE Estimate, NO OBLIGATIONS" right under the H1. Trimline and Mauro's lead with a phone number above the fold. Broke & Fixed's CTA needs the same density and the same friction-killing copy.
- **Build a testimonial wall and a project gallery page like Trebor.** Trebor's gallery + dedicated testimonial page is the single biggest legacy trust signal moving them despite a dated 2010-era site.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Build a `/services/[service]/[city]` URL matrix.** 6 services × 17 cities = 102 pages. Start with 6 highest-priority combos (bathroom + kitchen in Kendall, Coral Gables, Pinecrest). Each page: 1200-1500 words, unique H1, FAQ schema, Service schema, BreadcrumbList, 3-5 local project photos, named neighborhood references (street names, landmarks, zip codes).
2. **Add the proof stat block on the homepage hero.** "X projects completed | Y years building in Miami | Z homes remodeled | 5.0 Google rating". Use real numbers, do not fake.
3. **Implement Aggregate Rating + first-party Review schema on every service and location page.** None of the six competitors do this properly. Massive lane.
4. **Create dedicated `/gallery` and `/reviews` pages.** Both rank as standalone URLs (Trebor pattern). Tag each gallery image with service + city alt text.
5. **Publish 4 cost-guide blog posts immediately.** "How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Miami 2026", "Kitchen remodel cost Kendall vs Coral Gables", "Average tile installation cost Miami", "Interior painting cost per room Miami". High volume, low local-competitor coverage.
6. **Build a 5-field inline form above the fold (MIDI pattern).** Name, phone, email, service dropdown, zip. Add "FREE Estimate, No Obligation" microcopy and a "we reply within 1 hour" promise.
7. **Replace any "general contractor" trust signal with "fully insured + family owned + zero subcontractors".** This is the honest angle Trebor and Miami Pro cannot match because they brag about licenses (correct per CLAUDE.md rules).

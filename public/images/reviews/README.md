# Review captures drop folder

Drop the proof screenshots here: Google Business profile reviews, the WhatsApp
message, the email. Any filename, any of jpg/jpeg/png.

What happens next: the reviews section quotes the REAL text from these captures
with first name and neighborhood, and links to the Google profile. Nothing gets
invented, and nothing goes on the page until the capture is in this folder.

Best reviews to pick are the ones that name an objection (price honesty, the
week timeline, the mess, trust), not the ones that just say "great job".

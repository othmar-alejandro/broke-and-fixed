# Local Citations & Directory Checklist

Per `competitor-gap-analysis.md`, the Miami remodeling SERPs are dominated by
directories (Yelp, Angi, Houzz, HomeAdvisor, BBB, Thumbtack). None of the top 6
competitors out-page us on-site, but they show up across these directories and
we mostly do not. Off-site citations are the highest-leverage move left. This is
the work the "Claude Code SEO" videos skip.

Owner action required: most of these need a real account, email/phone
verification, and sometimes a postcard. Claude cannot create these. Work top to
bottom.

---

## Rule 0: NAP must be byte-for-byte identical everywhere

Name, Address, Phone (NAP) consistency is the single biggest local-citation
ranking factor. Copy-paste these exactly. Do not abbreviate "Solutions",
do not reformat the phone, do not vary the city.

```
Name:     Broke & Fixed Home Solutions
Phone:    (786) 363-7039
Email:    brokeandfixed305@gmail.com
Website:  https://brokeandfixed.com
City:     Kendall, FL 33186  (service-area business, hide street address)
Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/brokeandfixed/
```

Other fields to reuse:
- Hours: confirm with owner before publishing (must match Google Business Profile).
- Service area: the 17 cities in CLAUDE.md.
- Short description (under 200 chars):
  > Family owned home remodeling company serving Miami-Dade. Bathrooms, kitchens,
  > tile, interior and exterior painting, exterior repairs. Fully insured.
  > Bilingual. Free quotes.

### Category selection (important, brand-compliant)
Many directories default their category list to "General Contractor" or ask if
you are "Licensed". Do NOT pick those. We are a remodeling company with general
liability insurance, not a licensed contractor (see CLAUDE.md). Use, in order of
availability:
- Remodeler / Home Remodeling
- Bathroom Remodeler
- Kitchen Remodeler
- Painter / Painting
- Tile Contractor only if the field is a fixed taxonomy value with no remodeler
  option; never volunteer "licensed contractor" in free-text.

If a directory forces a "license number" field, leave it blank or mark N/A.
Never invent or imply a contractor license.

## After listings are live

1. Audit NAP consistency. Search `"Broke & Fixed Home Solutions"` and confirm
   every result shows the identical phone and city. Fix mismatches first; a
   wrong old phone number on one directory drags the whole profile down.
2. Build review velocity. QR code on invoices linking to the Google review URL
   (`https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ7VSW8vHbAgMRvNWjsiV5kII`),
   plus a GoHighLevel SMS 24h after job completion. Reviews are earned and
   third-party, which is exactly why we removed self-serving rating schema from
   the site.
3. Link directories back to the matching service page where the profile allows
   a website field (e.g. point the Yelp "Bathroom Remodeling" angle at
   `/en/services/bathroom-remodeling`).
4. Re-check quarterly. Directories drift and scrape stale data.

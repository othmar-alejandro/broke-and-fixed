## At a Glance: Bathroom Remodeling Costs in Miami (2026)

A standard bathroom remodel in Miami-Dade County costs between $8,500 and $25,000 in 2026. A half-bath refresh starts around $4,500. A full primary bathroom gut-and-rebuild can run $30,000 or more depending on materials and layout changes. Labor, tile, fixtures, and South Florida's humidity requirements all affect your final number.

## What Drives Bathroom Remodeling Costs in Miami-Dade?

Every bathroom remodel is different. A homeowner in Kendall replacing tile and a vanity pays a lot less than someone in Coral Gables tearing out walls to expand a shower. Here are the main cost factors:

**Scope of work.** Are you keeping the same layout or moving plumbing? Moving a toilet or shower drain adds $1,500 to $4,000 in plumbing work alone.

**Materials.** Porcelain tile at $3 per square foot looks very different from large-format natural stone at $15 per square foot. Your material choices can double the total cost.

**Bathroom size.** A 40 square foot half-bath costs a fraction of a 100+ square foot primary bathroom.

**Condition of existing space.** Older homes in areas like South Miami Heights or Westchester sometimes have moisture damage behind walls. That adds demo and repair costs you won't know about until walls come down.

**Permits.** Miami-Dade County requires permits for plumbing and electrical changes. Budget $200 to $800 depending on scope.

## Why Miami Bathrooms Cost More Than the National Average

South Florida adds specific challenges that affect your budget:

**Humidity and moisture.** Every bathroom in Miami needs proper waterproofing. Schluter or RedGard membrane systems are not optional here. They add $500 to $1,500 to a shower install, but they prevent mold and water damage behind your walls.

**Concrete block construction.** Most Miami homes are CBS (concrete block and stucco). Running new plumbing or electrical through concrete costs more than wood-frame construction up north.

**Material transport.** Miami is at the end of the supply chain for building materials. Tile, stone, and fixtures cost 10% to 20% more here compared to markets closer to distribution hubs.

**Demand.** The remodeling market in Miami-Dade stays busy year-round. No winter slowdown means labor rates stay consistent.

## How Long Does a Bathroom Remodel Take?

Timeline depends on scope:

- Budget refresh (no plumbing changes): 3 to 5 days
- Mid-range remodel: 2 to 3 weeks
- Full renovation with layout changes: 4 to 6 weeks

Add 1 to 2 weeks if you need permits. Miami-Dade permit processing takes 5 to 15 business days depending on complexity.

## How to Save Money on Your Bathroom Remodel

**Keep the same layout.** The moment you move a toilet or shower drain, costs jump $2,000+. If your current layout works, keep plumbing where it is.

**Choose porcelain over natural stone.** Modern porcelain tiles look identical to marble or travertine at a third of the cost. And porcelain handles Miami's humidity better.

**Skip the tub-to-shower conversion if your tub is in good shape.** Refinishing a bathtub costs $400 to $600 compared to $3,000+ for a full shower conversion.

**Get multiple estimates.** Prices vary 20% to 40% between remodeling teams in Miami-Dade. Get at least three written estimates that break down labor and materials separately.

**Buy your own fixtures.** Vanities, faucets, and lighting from Home Depot or Floor & Decor in Kendall often cost less than what a remodeling team marks up from their suppliers.

## Signs You Need a Bathroom Remodel (Not Just a Refresh)

Not every bathroom needs a gut renovation. Here is when a full remodel makes sense:

- Persistent mold or mildew that keeps returning after cleaning
- Cracked or loose tiles with water seeping underneath
- Outdated plumbing (galvanized pipes in pre-1980 homes)
- Layout that wastes space or does not fit your household
- Water stains on the ceiling below (sign of shower pan failure)

If you just want an updated look and your plumbing works fine, a $5,000 to $8,000 refresh handles it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a small bathroom remodel cost in Miami?

A small bathroom (under 50 sq ft) typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 in Miami-Dade depending on scope. A simple refresh with new vanity, paint, and fixtures runs $4,500 to $6,000. Full tile replacement and fixture upgrades push it to $8,000 to $12,000. Keeping the existing layout saves the most money.

### Can I remodel a bathroom for under $10,000 in Miami?

Yes. Focus on cosmetic updates: new vanity, updated fixtures, fresh paint, and re-caulking. Avoid moving plumbing or replacing all tile. A focused refresh that keeps the existing layout can deliver a noticeable transformation for $5,000 to $9,000 in most Miami-Dade neighborhoods.

### How much does tile installation cost in Miami bathrooms?

Tile installation in Miami-Dade runs $8 to $30 per square foot installed, depending on the tile material and pattern complexity. Standard porcelain floor tile is $8 to $15 per sq ft. Shower walls with niches and patterns run $15 to $30 per sq ft. A typical 60 sq ft bathroom floor costs $500 to $900 for tile installation alone.

### Do I need a permit for bathroom remodeling in Miami-Dade?

Miami-Dade County requires permits for any plumbing or electrical changes. A cosmetic refresh (paint, vanity swap, new fixtures on existing connections) usually does not require a permit. Moving a toilet, adding a shower, or running new electrical circuits requires permits and inspections. Budget $200 to $800 for permits.

### How long does a bathroom remodel take in South Florida?

A cosmetic refresh takes 3 to 5 days. A mid-range remodel with new tile, fixtures, and vanity takes 2 to 3 weeks. A full gut renovation with layout changes takes 4 to 6 weeks. Add 1 to 2 weeks for permit processing if your project requires one.

### What is the best time of year to remodel a bathroom in Miami?

Miami's remodeling market stays busy year-round, so there is no dramatic off-season discount. However, late spring (May to June) and early fall (September to October) tend to be slightly less busy. Hurricane season (June to November) can cause scheduling delays if storms hit, but indoor bathroom work is generally unaffected.

# Exterior Painting Cost in Miami: 2026 Price Guide

Your home's exterior takes a beating in South Florida. Between the UV rays, salt air, afternoon storms, and humidity, paint breaks down faster here than almost anywhere else in the country. Knowing what exterior painting actually costs in Miami-Dade helps you budget smart and avoid getting ripped off.

This guide covers real pricing from 2026 jobs across Miami-Dade County. No fluff. Just numbers.

## At a Glance: What Does Exterior Painting Cost in Miami?

Most Miami-Dade homeowners pay **$3,000 to $8,000** for a full exterior paint job on a single-story stucco home. The exact price depends on your home's size, condition, surface type, and the paint you choose.

Here's the quick breakdown by home size:

| Home Size (sq ft) | Price Range |
|---|---|
| 1,000 sq ft | $2,500 - $4,500 |
| 1,500 sq ft | $3,500 - $6,000 |
| 2,000 sq ft | $4,500 - $8,000 |
| 2,500+ sq ft | $6,000 - $12,000+ |

Per square foot of paintable exterior surface, expect to pay **$1.50 to $4.00**. That range covers everything from basic one-color jobs to detailed multi-color work with accent trim.

## Cost Breakdown by Home Size

### 1,000 Square Foot Homes ($2,500 - $4,500)

Smaller homes, townhomes, and duplexes fall in this range. A straightforward stucco home with minimal prep work sits closer to $2,500. Add in repairs, multiple colors, or detailed trim work and you'll push toward $4,500.

### 1,500 Square Foot Homes ($3,500 - $6,000)

This is the sweet spot for a lot of Miami-Dade neighborhoods. Think Kendall, Cutler Bay, The Hammocks. A typical single-story CBS (concrete block and stucco) home in decent shape runs about $4,000 to $5,000 for a quality two-coat job.

### 2,000 Square Foot Homes ($4,500 - $8,000)

Larger single-story and smaller two-story homes land here. The price jump usually comes from more paintable surface area and the added complexity of taller walls or detailed architectural features.

### 2,500+ Square Foot Homes ($6,000 - $12,000+)

Bigger homes in areas like [Palmetto Bay](/en/services/exterior-painting/palmetto-bay), Pinecrest, and Coral Gables often hit the top of this range. More square footage, more windows to mask, more trim, more time. Two-story homes with detailed fascia and soffits easily reach $10,000 or more.

## Two-Story Homes: The Scaffolding Factor

If your home is two stories, add 20% to 30% to the base price. That extra cost covers scaffolding or lift rental, additional safety measures, and the slower pace of painting at height.

A single-story home that would cost $5,000 might run $6,000 to $6,500 as a two-story. The paint and prep are similar. The access is what costs more.

## What Affects Your Exterior Painting Cost

### Home Size and Layout

Bigger home means more paint, more labor, more time. But layout matters too. A home with lots of corners, bump-outs, and architectural details takes longer than a simple rectangular floor plan.

### Number of Stories

As mentioned, two-story homes cost more. Equipment rental, safety setup, and slower progress all add up.

### Surface Condition

This is where costs can surprise you. A home that was last painted three years ago needs minimal prep. A home with peeling paint, cracks in the stucco, water stains, or mildew buildup needs serious prep work before a single coat goes on.

Stucco patching alone can add $300 to $1,500 depending on how much repair is needed.

### Surface Type

**Stucco** is by far the most common exterior surface in Miami-Dade. It's textured, which means it absorbs more paint than smooth surfaces. A full stucco exterior typically costs **$3,000 to $8,000** depending on the home's size.

**Wood trim** (fascia, soffits, window frames) requires scraping, sanding, and priming. Wood prep is labor-intensive and adds to the total.

**Concrete block** (exposed, unpainted) needs a block filler primer before paint. That extra step adds material cost and time.

**Aluminum or vinyl accents** (shutters, gutters, railings) can be painted but require specific primers and paints. Most painting teams include these in the overall quote.

### Prep Work Needed

Good prep is the difference between paint that lasts two years and paint that lasts seven. Here's what professional prep includes:

- **Pressure washing**: Removes dirt, mildew, loose paint, and debris. This is non-negotiable in Miami's humid climate.
- **Scraping and sanding**: Gets rid of peeling or flaking paint down to a solid surface.
- **Stucco repair**: Fills cracks, holes, and damaged areas.
- **Caulking**: Seals gaps around windows, doors, and joints. Keeps water out.
- **Priming**: Bare spots, repaired areas, and stain-prone zones get primer before topcoat.

Homes that have been neglected for years can add $500 to $2,000 in prep costs alone.

### Paint Quality

Not all paint is the same. Here's what the tiers look like:

**Economy paint ($25-$35/gallon)**: Gets the job done but fades faster in Miami's sun. Expect 3 to 4 years before it starts looking tired.

**Mid-range paint ($40-$55/gallon)**: Good balance of durability and cost. Brands like Sherwin-Williams SuperPaint or Benjamin Moore Regal fall here. Lasts 5 to 7 years in South Florida conditions.

**Premium paint ($55-$80/gallon)**: Top-tier coatings like Sherwin-Williams Duration or Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior. Better UV resistance, better mildew resistance, better coverage. Can last 7 to 10 years even in Miami's harsh climate.

The paint itself might only account for $300 to $800 of the total job. But cheaper paint means you'll be repainting sooner. Spending an extra $300 on premium paint that lasts three more years is almost always worth it.

### Number of Colors

A single-color exterior is the most cost-effective. Adding a second color for trim bumps the price up slightly, maybe $200 to $500. Three or more colors with detailed accent work can add $500 to $1,500.

### Trim and Accent Work

Painting trim, fascia, soffits, shutters, doors, and garage doors takes precision and extra time. Detailed trim work on a large home can be a significant portion of the total cost.

## What's Included in a Professional Exterior Paint Job

A reputable painting team should include all of this in their quote:

1. **Pressure washing** the entire exterior
2. **Scraping** loose and peeling paint
3. **Minor stucco patching** and crack repair
4. **Caulking** around windows, doors, and joints
5. **Priming** bare and repaired areas
6. **Masking and protecting** windows, doors, landscaping, walkways, and fixtures
7. **Two coats of exterior paint** on all surfaces
8. **Cleanup** of all materials, tarps, and debris

If a quote doesn't include pressure washing or only offers one coat, that's a red flag. One coat might look okay for a few months, but it won't hold up to Miami weather.

## Surface-Specific Considerations for Miami Homes

### Stucco (The Miami Standard)

About 80% of homes in Miami-Dade have stucco exteriors. Stucco is durable but porous. It soaks up paint, especially if it hasn't been painted in a while or if the existing paint is worn thin.

Key things to know about painting stucco in Miami:

- Hairline cracks are normal and should be filled with elastomeric caulk before painting.
- Larger cracks (over 1/4 inch) need proper stucco patching.
- Elastomeric paint is worth considering for stucco. It stretches with the surface and bridges small cracks. It costs more but prevents water intrusion.
- Pressure washing stucco before painting is critical. Miami's humidity breeds mildew that hides in the texture.

### Wood Trim and Details

Even on stucco homes, you'll find wood fascia boards, soffits, window frames, and door trim. Wood in Miami's climate takes a beating from moisture and termites.

Before painting, all wood should be inspected for rot. Replacing a few boards during the paint job is cheaper than dealing with water damage later.

### Concrete Block (CBS Construction)

Older Miami homes and additions often have exposed concrete block. Unpainted block needs a block filler primer to fill the porous surface before topcoat. This adds about $0.50 to $1.00 per square foot to the job.

### Aluminum and Vinyl Accents

Hurricane shutters, aluminum railings, vinyl soffits, and gutters can all be painted. They require deglossing or light sanding and a bonding primer. Most painting teams handle these as part of the overall exterior job.

## Miami-Specific Factors That Affect Exterior Paint

Living in South Florida means your exterior paint faces conditions that homes in other parts of the country never deal with.

### UV Exposure

Miami gets intense, direct sunlight year-round. UV rays break down paint pigments and resins faster here than in northern climates. South and west-facing walls fade the fastest. Dark colors fade more noticeably than lighter ones.

This is why paint quality matters so much in Miami. A premium paint with high UV resistance pays for itself over time.

### Humidity and Mildew

Miami's humidity is relentless. Mildew and algae grow on exterior surfaces constantly, especially on north-facing walls and shaded areas. Mildew-resistant exterior paint is a must, not an upgrade. Any painting team working in Miami should be using paint formulated for high-humidity environments.

### Salt Air

Homes within a few miles of the coast deal with salt air corrosion. Salt accelerates paint breakdown and metal corrosion. Coastal homes may need repainting a year or two sooner than inland homes. Using a high-quality acrylic latex paint helps resist salt damage.

### CBS Construction

Most Miami-Dade homes are CBS (concrete block and stucco). This construction is great for hurricanes but creates specific painting considerations. The concrete block absorbs moisture, and stucco can crack as the structure settles. Proper surface prep addresses both issues.

### Hurricane Shutters and Hardware

Accordion shutters, panel tracks, bolts, and mounting hardware all need to be worked around during an exterior paint job. Some shutters need to be removed and reinstalled. Others get masked off and painted around. Either way, shutters add time and complexity to the job.

## How to Save Money on Exterior Painting

### Do Some Prep Yourself

Pressure washing your home before the painting team arrives can save $200 to $400. You can rent a pressure washer for about $75 per day. Just be careful with stucco. Too much pressure damages the surface.

### Choose Fewer Colors

Sticking with one body color and one trim color is the most cost-effective approach. Every additional color adds masking time and labor.

### Paint During the Off-Season

Miami's painting "slow season" runs roughly from June through September (hurricane season and the rainiest months). Some painting companies offer discounts during this period to keep crews busy. Just know that rain delays can stretch the timeline.

### Maintain Your Paint

Pressure washing your home once a year extends the life of your exterior paint significantly. Removing mildew and dirt before they break down the paint surface adds years to the job.

### Bundle Services

If you need other exterior work done (trim repair, stucco patching, [exterior repairs](/en/services/exterior-painting/kendall)), bundling everything with one company often saves money compared to hiring separate crews.

### Get Multiple Quotes

Always get at least three quotes. But don't just pick the cheapest one. Compare what's included. A $3,000 quote with one coat and no prep is not the same as a $4,500 quote with full prep and two coats.

## FAQ: Exterior Painting Cost in Miami

### How much does it cost to paint a stucco house in Miami?

Most stucco homes in Miami-Dade cost between $3,000 and $8,000 for a full exterior paint job. The price depends on the home's size, the condition of the existing stucco, how much prep work is needed, and the quality of paint you choose. Single-story homes under 2,000 square feet typically fall in the $3,500 to $6,000 range.

### How often should you repaint your home's exterior in Miami?

In Miami's climate, plan on repainting every 5 to 7 years with mid-range paint, or 7 to 10 years with premium paint. South and west-facing walls fade faster and may need attention sooner. Regular pressure washing once a year helps extend the time between paint jobs by preventing mildew and dirt buildup.

### Is one coat of exterior paint enough in Miami?

No. One coat might look fine for a few months, but it won't hold up to Miami's UV exposure, rain, and humidity. Two coats is the standard for any quality exterior paint job. Two coats provide better coverage, richer color, and significantly longer durability. Any estimate offering just one coat should raise concerns.

### What's the best time of year to paint a house exterior in Miami?

October through May is the ideal window. Lower humidity, less rain, and milder temperatures create the best conditions for paint adhesion and curing. Painting during summer isn't impossible, but rain delays are common and high humidity can affect how the paint dries and bonds to the surface.

### Should I use elastomeric paint on my stucco home?

Elastomeric paint is a solid choice for stucco homes in Miami. It creates a thicker, flexible coating that bridges hairline cracks and provides excellent water resistance. It costs more than standard acrylic latex (roughly 30% to 50% more) but offers superior protection against water intrusion, which is a real concern with South Florida's driving rain.

### Can I paint my house exterior myself to save money?

You can, but a full exterior paint job is a big undertaking. It requires proper equipment (ladders, scaffolding, sprayers), surface prep knowledge, and physical endurance. Most DIY exterior jobs take 3 to 5 weekends. The risk of uneven coverage, missed prep steps, and safety issues often makes hiring a professional painting team the smarter long-term investment.

## Que Afecta el Costo de su Pintura Exterior

### Tamano y Diseno de la Casa

Casa mas grande significa mas pintura, mas mano de obra, mas tiempo. Pero el diseno tambien importa. Una casa con muchas esquinas, salientes y detalles arquitectonicos toma mas tiempo que un plano rectangular simple.

### Numero de Pisos

Como se menciono, las casas de dos pisos cuestan mas. El alquiler de equipos, la configuracion de seguridad y el progreso mas lento se suman.

### Condicion de la Superficie

Aqui es donde los costos pueden sorprenderle. Una casa que se pinto hace tres anos necesita minima preparacion. Una casa con pintura descascarada, grietas en el estuco, manchas de agua o acumulacion de moho necesita un trabajo serio de preparacion antes de que se aplique una sola capa.

Solo el parcheo de estuco puede agregar $300 a $1,500 dependiendo de cuanta reparacion se necesite.

### Tipo de Superficie

**Estuco** es con mucho la superficie exterior mas comun en Miami-Dade. Es texturizado, lo que significa que absorbe mas pintura que las superficies lisas. Un exterior completo de estuco tipicamente cuesta **$3,000 a $8,000** dependiendo del tamano de la casa.

**Molduras de madera** (fascia, sofitos, marcos de ventanas) requieren raspado, lijado e imprimacion. La preparacion de madera es intensiva en mano de obra y agrega al total.

**Bloque de concreto** (expuesto, sin pintar) necesita un primer de relleno para bloques antes de pintar. Ese paso adicional agrega costo de materiales y tiempo.

**Acentos de aluminio o vinilo** (persianas, canaletas, barandas) se pueden pintar pero requieren primers y pinturas especificas. La mayoria de los equipos de pintura incluyen estos en la cotizacion general.

### Trabajo de Preparacion Necesario

Una buena preparacion es la diferencia entre pintura que dura dos anos y pintura que dura siete. Esto es lo que incluye la preparacion profesional:

- **Lavado a presion**: Remueve suciedad, moho, pintura suelta y escombros. Esto no es negociable en el clima humedo de Miami.
- **Raspado y lijado**: Elimina la pintura descascarada o escamosa hasta una superficie solida.
- **Reparacion de estuco**: Rellena grietas, agujeros y areas danadas.
- **Calafateo**: Sella espacios alrededor de ventanas, puertas y juntas. Mantiene el agua fuera.
- **Imprimacion**: Las areas expuestas, reparadas y propensas a manchas reciben primer antes de la capa final.

Las casas que han sido descuidadas por anos pueden agregar $500 a $2,000 solo en costos de preparacion.

### Calidad de la Pintura

No toda la pintura es igual. Asi se ven los niveles:

**Pintura economica ($25-$35/galon)**: Hace el trabajo pero se desvanece mas rapido bajo el sol de Miami. Espere 3 a 4 anos antes de que empiece a verse cansada.

**Pintura de rango medio ($40-$55/galon)**: Buen balance de durabilidad y costo. Marcas como Sherwin-Williams SuperPaint o Benjamin Moore Regal caen aqui. Dura 5 a 7 anos en condiciones del sur de Florida.

**Pintura premium ($55-$80/galon)**: Recubrimientos de primera como Sherwin-Williams Duration o Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior. Mejor resistencia UV, mejor resistencia al moho, mejor cobertura. Puede durar 7 a 10 anos incluso en el clima severo de Miami.

La pintura en si podria representar solo $300 a $800 del trabajo total. Pero la pintura mas barata significa que repintara antes. Gastar $300 extra en pintura premium que dura tres anos mas casi siempre vale la pena.

### Numero de Colores

Un exterior de un solo color es lo mas economico. Agregar un segundo color para molduras aumenta el precio ligeramente, quizas $200 a $500. Tres o mas colores con trabajo de acento detallado pueden agregar $500 a $1,500.

### Trabajo de Molduras y Acentos

Pintar molduras, fascia, sofitos, persianas, puertas y puertas de garaje requiere precision y tiempo extra. El trabajo detallado de molduras en una casa grande puede ser una porcion significativa del costo total.

Las persianas contra huracanes, las barandas de aluminio, los sofitos de vinilo y las canaletas pueden pintarse. Requieren desbrillado o lijado ligero y un primer de adherencia. La mayoria de los equipos de pintura manejan estos como parte del trabajo exterior general.

La humedad de Miami es implacable. El moho y las algas crecen en superficies exteriores constantemente, especialmente en paredes orientadas al norte y areas sombreadas. La pintura exterior resistente al moho es una necesidad, no una mejora. Cualquier equipo de pintura que trabaje en Miami deberia usar pintura formulada para ambientes de alta humedad.

Las casas a pocas millas de la costa lidian con la corrosion del aire salado. La sal acelera el deterioro de la pintura y la corrosion del metal. ## Preguntas Frecuentes: Costo de Pintura Exterior en Miami

### Cuanto cuesta pintar una casa de estuco en Miami?

La mayoria de las casas de estuco en Miami-Dade cuestan entre $3,000 y $8,000 por un trabajo completo de pintura exterior. El precio depende del tamano de la casa, la condicion del estuco existente, cuanta preparacion se necesita y la calidad de pintura que elija. Las casas de un piso de menos de 2,000 pies cuadrados tipicamente caen en el rango de $3,500 a $6,000.

### Cada cuanto debe repintar el exterior de su casa en Miami?

En el clima de Miami, planee repintar cada 5 a 7 anos con pintura de rango medio, o 7 a 10 anos con pintura premium. Las paredes orientadas al sur y al oeste se desvanecen mas rapido y pueden necesitar atencion antes. El lavado a presion regular una vez al ano ayuda a extender el tiempo entre trabajos de pintura previniendo la acumulacion de moho y suciedad.

### Es suficiente una capa de pintura exterior en Miami?

No. Una capa podria verse bien por unos meses, pero no resistira la exposicion UV, la lluvia y la humedad de Miami. Dos capas es el estandar para cualquier trabajo de pintura exterior de calidad. Dos capas proporcionan mejor cobertura, color mas rico y durabilidad significativamente mas larga. Cualquier presupuesto que ofrezca solo una capa deberia generar preocupacion.

### Cual es la mejor epoca del ano para pintar el exterior de una casa en Miami?

Octubre a mayo es la ventana ideal. Menor humedad, menos lluvia y temperaturas mas moderadas crean las mejores condiciones para la adhesion y el curado de la pintura. Pintar durante el verano no es imposible, pero los retrasos por lluvia son comunes y la alta humedad puede afectar como la pintura se seca y se adhiere a la superficie.

### Deberia usar pintura elastomerica en mi casa de estuco?

La pintura elastomerica es una opcion solida para casas de estuco en Miami. Crea un recubrimiento mas grueso y flexible que cubre grietas finas y proporciona excelente resistencia al agua. Cuesta mas que el latex acrilico estandar (aproximadamente 30% a 50% mas) pero ofrece proteccion superior contra la infiltracion de agua, lo cual es una preocupacion real con las lluvias fuertes del sur de Florida.

### Puedo pintar el exterior de mi casa yo mismo para ahorrar dinero?

Puede, pero un trabajo completo de pintura exterior es una tarea grande. Requiere equipo adecuado (escaleras, andamios, pistolas de pintura), conocimiento de preparacion de superficies y resistencia fisica. La mayoria de los trabajos exteriores hechos por uno mismo toman de 3 a 5 fines de semana. El riesgo de cobertura desigual, pasos de preparacion omitidos y problemas de seguridad a menudo hace que contratar un equipo de pintura profesional sea la mejor inversion a largo plazo.

## STORIES SEQUENCE (post the same day)

Run these as a 6-frame Story arc. Stories keep you at the top of followers' feeds and drive engagement that boosts the Reel.

|-------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Front door + paper down | "Demo day. Glenvar Heights." |
| 2 | Hallway paper run | "Floor protection from the door to the bathroom 👇" |
| 3 | Bathroom BEFORE | "Before" + poll sticker: "Keep the stone or change it?" |
| 4 | Mid-demo chaos | "After 4 hours" |
| 5 | Team at the studs | Question sticker: "Ask us anything about your remodel" |
| 6 | Final wide of demo'd bathroom | "Phase 1 ✅ Plumbing next" + link sticker to website/quote form |

**Why this arc works:** It mirrors the Reel beat-for-beat, but the poll and question stickers in frames 3 and 5 drive DMs. DMs are the strongest engagement signal Instagram tracks. The link sticker in frame 6 is where you capture intent.

- **First comment.** Drop the hashtag block as the first comment within 60 seconds of posting. Pin it.
- **First hour matters.** Reply to every comment in the first hour. Even "🔥" deserves a reply that's at least 4 words ("Appreciate it, more coming Monday").
- **Cross-post.** Same Reel goes on TikTok and Facebook Reels the same day. Different audiences, free reach.
- **Save for the case study.** These photos belong in the bathroom remodeling service page on the site under a "Glenvar Heights case study" entry once the project finishes. The Phase 1 → 4 series is also a strong blog post.
